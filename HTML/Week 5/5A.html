Valorant

Valorant is a popular 5v5 character-based tactical first-person shooter developed by Riot Games. It combines precise, high-stakes gunplay with unique agent abilities, requiring strategic teamwork and fast thinking across 13-round halves. Players can choose from a diverse roster of agents to outplay opponents in various competitive and casual modes.

Counter-Strike 2

Counter-Strike 2 (CS2) is a premier tactical first-person shooter developed by Valve, released on September 27, 2023, as a free-to-play, direct successor to Counter-Strike: Global Offensive. Built on the Source 2 engine, the game features major technical upgrades including revamped, high-fidelity maps, overhauled lighting, and a new "sub-tick" server architecture designed for better gameplay responsiveness. While it maintains the core 5v5 bomb-defusal gameplay, it introduces dynamic, volumetric smoke grenades that interact with the environment.

Fortnite

Fortnite, developed by Epic Games and released in 2017, is a massively popular free-to-play battle royale game that has become a major cultural phenomenon. In the primary game mode, 100 players skydive onto an island, scavenging for weapons and resources to be the last person or team standing while avoiding a shrinking "storm". A key differentiator from other shooters is the ability to instantly build structures for defense or tactical advantage, along with a bright, cartoonish art style that appeals to a wide audience.
