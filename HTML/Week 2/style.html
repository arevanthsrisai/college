Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, also known as Amrita University, is a multi-disciplinary, research-intensive, private university in India.
            
It was founded in 2003 by Mata Amritanandamayi Devi, a renowned spiritual leader and humanitarian.
    
The university has campuses in Amritapuri (Kerala), Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu), Bengaluru (Karnataka), Chennai (Tamil Nadu), and Kochi (Kerala).

Amrita offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across various disciplines,
including engineering, management, medical sciences, Ayurveda, life sciences, physical sciences,
agricultural sciences, law, arts & humanities, and social & behavioral sciences.

Amrita is known for its emphasis on research and innovation, with numerous research centers and collaborations with leading institutions worldwide.
